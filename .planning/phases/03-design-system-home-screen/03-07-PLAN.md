  <name>Task 1: Visual and interactive verification of the redesigned home screen</name>
  <what-built>
    Plans 01–06 delivered: design token system (AppColors, AppTypography, AppSpacing), SkyState enum, bortleClass → astrZone rename, SkyStateBackground widget, HeroConditionLabel, ConditionsCard (with Explore button), CloudBar restyle, VisibilityMiniCard, MoonMiniCard, HighlightsFeed restyle, ScaffoldWithNavBar token migration, and global DS-02 token sweep across all 15 surfaces.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Run the app on an iOS Simulator or Android Emulator in debug mode: flutter run

    Step 1 — Sky state background (HOME-01):
    - The home screen shows a sky photograph as the full-bleed background.
    - Temporarily force-set the sky state (or use a location with known conditions) to cycle through all 6 states. Confirm each shows the correct image:
      - Excellent → milky way photograph (stars + milky way band)
      - Good → starry sky photograph
      - Fair → planets photograph
      - Poor → few stars photograph
      - Cloudy → cloudy sky photograph
      - Too Much Light → light-polluted photograph
    - Loading state: should show starry sky photograph until data resolves.

    Step 2 — Hero section (HOME-02, HOME-03):
    - Hero label is large (72px, visually dominant), white text, centered.
    - Below hero label: sky state subtitle (e.g. "Milkyway Visible"), smaller text, centered.
    - Below subtitle: Zone badge pill — dark semi-transparent background, blue border, "Zone N" text.

    Step 3 — Conditions card (HOME-04):
    - Conditions card visible below hero section, with a dark glass appearance (slight transparency, blur visible).
    - Card shows: title (e.g. "Clear Skies"), small subtitle text, cloud cover bar (blue fill, glow effect), 4 sun/moon time cells (SUNRISE, SUNSET, MOONRISE, MOONSET) each showing a time or "--:--".
    - "Explore" button visible in the top-right corner of the card (blue fill, 100×27px).

    Step 4 — Explore button (HOME-09):
    - Tap the "Explore" button.
    - AtmosphericsSheet opens as a bottom sheet (slides up from bottom).
    - Dismiss the sheet (swipe down or tap outside).

    Step 5 — Visibility mini-card (HOME-05):
    - Left half of the mini-cards row shows VISIBILITY card.
    - "VISIBILITY" label visible (small, muted, uppercase).
    - Sky type label visible (e.g. "Rural Sky", large text, ~28px visual size).
    - Zone pill visible in top-right of card (blue tinted pill, "Zone N").
    - 5 rating bars visible at the bottom of the card.

    Step 6 — Moon mini-card (HOME-06):
    - Right half of the mini-cards row shows MOON card.
    - "MOON" label visible (small, muted, uppercase, top-left).
    - Moon illumination % visible (top-right, e.g. "65%").
    - Moon phase image visible centered.
    - Phase name visible (small text, bottom area).

    Step 7 — Date navigation (HOME-07):
    - Tap the "<" chevron in the header. Date in the date pill decreases by 1 day.
    - Tap the ">" chevron. Date in the date pill increases by 1 day.
    - Tap past the 10-day boundary — a glass toast should appear.
    - Tap the date pill itself — date picker should open.

    Step 8 — Location pill (HOME-08):
    - Tap the "Current Location" pill in the header.
    - LocationSheet opens as a bottom sheet.
    - Dismiss the sheet.

    Step 9 — HighlightsFeed (D-15):
    - "TONIGHT'S HIGHLIGHTS" section label visible below mini-cards (uppercase, muted).
    - Object rows visible with orange icon box, object name, best view time.
    - Tap any row — CelestialDetailSheet opens.

    Step 10 — Design system (DS-02):
    - Confirm no obviously "off-brand" colors visible: no bright purple, no bright white that looks out of place, no blueAccent that doesn't fit the design.
    - Dark starfield aesthetic is consistent across all visible elements.
    - Nav bar uses the correct dark glass appearance with blue FAB.

    Compare against figma/home/Home.png for layout and visual reference.
  </how-to-verify>
