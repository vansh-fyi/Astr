## Phase Goal

**As a** stargazer, **I want to** open Astr and see tonight's sky conditions on a fully redesigned home screen, **so that** I know instantly whether it's worth going outside.

<objective>
Deliver the first user-visible MVP slice of the redesigned home screen: a full-bleed illustrated background image matching the current sky state, a large 72px hero condition label, a sky state subtitle, and a zone badge pill. After this plan, a user running the app sees a completely new visual experience — the old nebula background is gone and the correct sky photograph is displayed.

Purpose: HOME-01 (correct illustrated background), HOME-02 (hero label), HOME-03 (zone badge) are all delivered here as the thinnest visible slice of the redesigned home screen. The conditions card and mini-cards sections are placeholder SizedBoxes in this wave.

Output: sky_state_background.dart (new), hero_condition_label.dart (new), home_screen.dart (refactored to Stack layout), nebula_background.dart + sky_portal.dart (imports removed), sky_state_background_test.dart (new).
</objective>
