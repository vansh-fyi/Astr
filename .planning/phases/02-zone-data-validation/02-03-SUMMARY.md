# Phase 02 Plan 03: Scatter Retune and zones.db Regeneration Summary

**One-liner:** Full accumulator reset + VNL re-scan with SCATTER_FRACTION=0.06 completed in 54 min (17 batches of 10 strips); zones.db regenerated at 715.8 MB with 37.5M records; validation holds at 14/25 PASS — Teide fixed, Anchorage regressed, 11 failures persist from VNL data quality and H3 coordinate placement issues.

## What Was Built

### Task 0 (prerequisite): Add --batch and --reset-accum flags to apply_skyglow.py

The Plan 03 execution discovered that the accumulator's `MAX()` SQL semantics make it a one-way ratchet — scatter values can never decrease without a full reset. To enable a fresh rebuild, two CLI flags were added:

- `--reset-accum`: Clears `cells` and `progress` tables from the accumulator before scanning
- `--batch N`: Processes N strips then exits with `[BATCH PAUSE]`, enabling incremental runs

Commit: `2bcee63` — `feat(02-03): add --batch and --reset-accum flags to apply_skyglow.py`

### Task 1: Select scatter parameter (pre-resolved checkpoint)

Developer selected option-a: SCATTER_FRACTION=0.06 (halve current value from 0.12).

Rationale: Halving scatter should bring over-scattered small cities (Reykjavik, Wellington, Ushuaia) down 1-2 zones while keeping major urban cores (NYC at 247 nW, London at 207 nW) well above Zone 9 threshold (125 nW).

## Root Cause Analysis of Persistent Failures

Failures fall into two categories with distinct causes:

### Category 1: H3 coordinate underestimates city cores (4 cities)

These cities return radiance well below the Zone 9 threshold (125 nW) because the test coordinate H3 cell samples a lower-density part of the city, not the hot commercial core:

| Location | Radiance | Zone | Gap to Zone 9 | Analysis |
|----------|----------|------|---------------|----------|
| Tokyo (35.6762, 139.6503) | 67.43 | 8 | −57.6 nW | Shinjuku/Shibuya hot core is a different H3 cell |
| Paris (48.8566, 2.3522) | 97.86 | 8 | −27.1 nW | Eiffel Tower area, not CBD core |
| Mumbai (19.0760, 72.8777) | 93.52 | 8 | −31.5 nW | Slightly off downtown |
| Singapore (1.3521, 103.8198) | 36.97 | 7 | −88.0 nW | Central area but VNL shows low equatorial reading |

These cannot be fixed by scatter parameter tuning. Options: adjust test coordinates to hit brighter H3 cells, or accept that Astr zone is accurate for the specific coordinate.

### Category 2: Scatter overestimates small/mid cities (7 cities)

These cities show radiance too high relative to Lorenz expectations. Even at fraction=0.06 the scatter from nearby or within-city pixels keeps radiance elevated:

| Location | Radiance | Zone | Expected | Delta |
|----------|----------|------|----------|-------|
| Reykjavik (64.1355, -21.8954) | 125.82 | 9 | 7 | +2 zones, possibly VNL noise at 65°N |
| Ushuaia (-54.8019, -68.3030) | 105.75 | 8 | 7 | +1 zone, scatter from tiny 55k city |
| Wellington (-41.2865, 174.7762) | 59.47 | 8 | 7 | +1 zone, scatter artifact |
| Cape Town (-33.9249, 18.4241) | 83.89 | 8 | 7 | +1 zone, possibly expected zone too low |
| Anchorage (61.2176, -149.8997) | 124.66 | 8 | 9 | −1 zone, 0.34 nW below Zone 9 threshold |
| Sydney (-33.8688, 151.2093) | 148.36 | 9 | 8 | +1 zone, expected zone may be wrong |
| Greenland Nuuk (64.1836, -51.7214) | 15.92 | 6 | 5 | +1 zone, 15.92 vs 9.0 threshold |

Reykjavik (125.82 nW for a 230k city) is likely a VNL 2024 artifact — high-latitude aurora contamination or coastal ice reflectance inflating the nighttime light reading. Cannot be reduced further by scatter tuning alone.

Anchorage at 124.66 nW is within 0.27% of the Zone 9 threshold (125.0). An intermediate fraction (0.07-0.08) would likely recover it.
