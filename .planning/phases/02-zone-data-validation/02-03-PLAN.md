    The Plan 01 mismatch table shows which of the 25 locations have wrong zones.
    The working hypothesis from RESEARCH.md is that SCATTER_FRACTION=0.12 is too high for
    small-to-medium cities (Reykjavik 230k, Anchorage 290k, Ushuaia 55k, Wellington 420k).
    Reducing to 0.06-0.08 should bring those cities down 1-2 zones while leaving major urban
    cores (NYC radiance ~284, London ~226) at Zone 9 regardless.

    requires another full re-run. The decision here determines the first (and hopefully only)
    re-scan value.

    SCATTER_SCALE_KM=20.0 and SCATTER_POWER=2.5 should remain unchanged unless the mismatch
    pattern suggests a range problem (misses affecting rural areas far from cities) rather than
    a magnitude problem (whole zones off).
