<DocSection id="formula" title="Formula">

Astronomers measure brightness in magnitudes. The Pogson relation says a difference of five magnitudes is exactly a factor of 100 in flux:

$$ m_2 - m_1 = -2.5\,\log_{10}\frac{F_2}{F_1} $$

so one magnitude is a ratio of $100^{1/5} \approx 2.512$. The ladder steps through alpha in half-magnitude increments:

$$ \alpha(n) = 10^{-0.4\,n/2} $$

Ten steps are five magnitudes, so $\alpha(10) = 10^{-2} = 1\%$ and the last step is exactly 100 times dimmer than the first. To find the nearest step for an arbitrary alpha, invert the relation:

$$ n = -5\,\log_{10}\alpha $$
